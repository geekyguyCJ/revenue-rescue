GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s talk

Do I need any new hardware?

No new phone or special hardware is needed. We work with your current phone setup and check the connection requirements before we begin.

Is there a monthly fee?

There’s a $500 one-time setup fee and no monthly fee from Revenue Rescue. You cover your own Twilio usage, billed separately. We walk you through how that works before setup.

How quickly can I get set up?

Timing depends on your current phone provider and any number or messaging verification needed. We review your setup first and confirm a timeline before starting.

Will it work with my current number?

The service is designed around your existing business number. We check your provider’s forwarding options and messaging compatibility first, then explain exactly how calls and texts will work.

What happens to my voicemail?

We review your voicemail and call-forwarding settings during setup. The aim is to keep a voicemail option while adding text follow-up. The exact call flow depends on your provider, and we test it with you before it goes live.
